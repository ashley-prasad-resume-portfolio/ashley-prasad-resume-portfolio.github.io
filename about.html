My Approach

Clients ALWAYS come first. I begin by scheduling an interview with my clients to see if we are compatible and capable of collaborating with each other. During the interview, I require a deep-understanding of what your project needs, what the desired outcome is, and of course to meet your needs and expectations throughout the development. At every step of the way you, the client, is always involved.

What I value

  • Clear and strong communication
  • Creating designs that are responsive and accessible
  • Honesty and transparency
  • Critical feedback. There is always room for more improvement.
